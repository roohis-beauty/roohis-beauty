FREQUENTLY ASKED

Everything you need to know about our products and ordering.

How do I place an order via WhatsApp?

Simply add your desired items to the bag, open your cart drawer, and click "Order on WhatsApp." You will be redirected to complete your order details directly with our team.

Are your products suitable for sensitive skin?

Yes, all our curated skincare products are formulated to be gentle on skin barriers. We recommend checking individual product descriptions for specific key active ingredients.

How long does delivery take?

Standard delivery within Dhaka takes 1-2 business days. Delivery outside Dhaka usually takes 3-5 business days.
